Data & Privacy

Your inventory and sales are stored in this browser. Export a backup regularly. Importing a backup replaces the current inventory and sales data.

Security note: This is a browser-only demo. Password verification is protected with PBKDF2 hashing, but this is not server-side authentication. Anyone with access to this browser profile or developer tools may access or alter locally stored data. Do not use real or sensitive business information without a secure backend and database.
